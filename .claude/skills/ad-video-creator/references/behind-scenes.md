# For Behind-the-Scenes
Structure the prompt with these components:

**15-30 seconds:**
1. Workspace Intro (5-8s) - Show the environment
2. Process Showcase (12-15s) - Quick making-of
3. Product Connection (5-8s) - How it relates to product
4. CTA (3-5s) - Join us/Learn more

**30-60 seconds:**
1. Team/Workspace Intro (8-12s) - Show people and environment
2. Process Showcase (20-25s) - Detailed making-of
3. Values/Mission (8-12s) - Why you do it
4. Product Connection (8-10s) - How process creates great product
5. CTA (5s) - Join us/Learn more

**60-90 seconds:**
1. Team/Workspace Intro (12-15s) - Meet the people
2. Process Showcase Part 1 (15-20s) - Early stages
3. Process Showcase Part 2 (15-20s) - Completion stages
4. Values/Mission (12-15s) - Company culture and why
5. Product Connection (10-12s) - How care translates to quality
6. CTA (5-8s) - Join us/Learn more
