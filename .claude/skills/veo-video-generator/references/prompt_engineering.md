# Veo 3 Prompt Engineering Guide

## Effective Prompt Structure

Veo 3 generates best results with detailed, structured prompts. Follow these patterns:

### Basic Structure

```
[Shot type/Camera angle] [Subject/Action] [Setting/Environment] [Lighting/Mood] [Additional details]
```

### Examples

**Good prompts:**
- "A close-up of two people staring at a cryptic drawing on a wall, torchlight flickering. A man murmurs, 'This must be it. That's the secret code.' The woman looks at him and whispering excitedly, 'What did you find?'"
- "Wide shot of a bustling city street at golden hour. People walking in slow motion, warm sunlight filtering through buildings, lens flare, cinematic look"
- "Overhead drone shot of a forest canopy, camera slowly descending through the trees, morning mist, soft ambient lighting, revealing a hidden clearing"

**Poor prompts:**
- "A street" (too vague)
- "Make a cool video" (no specific details)
- "Video of a person" (lacks context, action, setting)

## Key Elements to Include

### 1. Camera Work
- **Shot types**: Close-up, wide shot, medium shot, extreme close-up
- **Camera movement**: Pan, tilt, zoom, tracking shot, dolly, crane shot, drone shot
- **Angles**: Low angle, high angle, bird's eye, Dutch angle, over-the-shoulder

Examples:
- "Slow tracking shot following a character"
- "Aerial drone view circling around"
- "Low angle shot looking up at"

### 2. Subject and Action
- Clearly describe who/what is in the scene
- Specify actions, movements, expressions
- Include dialogue if relevant (Veo 3 supports text-to-speech)

Examples:
- "A woman in a red coat walks briskly"
- "Two children laughing and playing catch"
- "A chef carefully plating a dish, hands in focus"

### 3. Environment and Setting
- Location description
- Time of day
- Weather conditions
- Background elements

Examples:
- "In a modern minimalist kitchen"
- "On a beach at sunset"
- "Inside a dimly lit jazz club, smoke in the air"

### 4. Lighting and Mood
- Light quality: harsh, soft, diffused
- Light direction: backlit, side-lit, front-lit
- Color temperature: warm, cool, neutral
- Atmosphere: moody, bright, mysterious

Examples:
- "Soft morning light filtering through windows"
- "Harsh neon lights reflecting off wet pavement"
- "Warm candlelight creating dancing shadows"

### 5. Visual Style
- Cinematic style references
- Color grading preferences
- Motion characteristics

Examples:
- "Film noir aesthetic, high contrast"
- "Pastel colors, whimsical feel"
- "Slow motion, dreamlike quality"

## Image-to-Video Prompts

When generating from an image, focus on:
- How the scene should animate
- Camera movement relative to the image
- What elements should move vs. stay static

**Examples:**
- "Camera slowly pushes in on the subject while background elements gently sway"
- "Animate the water flowing, leaves rustling, while the main subject remains still"
- "Pan across the landscape from left to right, revealing more of the scene"

## Common Pitfalls

❌ **Too generic**: "A beautiful sunset"
✅ **Better**: "Time-lapse of a vibrant orange and purple sunset over calm ocean waters, wispy clouds moving across the sky"

❌ **Conflicting instructions**: "Fast-paced action in slow motion"
✅ **Better**: "Action sequence captured in dramatic slow motion, 120fps"

❌ **Too many elements**: "A person walking a dog while riding a bike juggling balls in a crowded market at sunset with fireworks"
✅ **Better**: Focus on 1-2 main elements with supporting details

## Optimization Tips

1. **Start specific, then refine**: Begin with detailed prompts and adjust based on results
2. **Use temporal language**: "begins with," "transitions to," "ends with" for narrative flow
3. **Reference cinematography**: Mention film styles, directors, or visual techniques if helpful
4. **Keep it natural**: Veo excels at realistic motion; avoid physically impossible scenarios unless intentional
5. **Dialogue clarity**: Use quotation marks for spoken text, specify tone/emotion

## Prompt Templates

### Cinematic Scene
```
[Camera movement] shot of [subject] [action] in [location]. [Lighting description]. [Mood/style]. [Additional details].
```

### Product Showcase
```
[Camera angle] of [product] on [surface/setting]. [Lighting type] highlights [product features]. [Camera movement] reveals [details]. [Style/aesthetic].
```

### Nature/Landscape
```
[Shot type] of [landscape/nature element]. [Time of day] lighting, [weather conditions]. [Camera movement]. [Atmosphere description].
```

### Character-Driven
```
[Shot type] of [character description] [performing action]. [Setting details]. [Character says/does]. [Emotional tone]. [Lighting/mood].
```
