# For Testimonials
Structure the prompt with these components:

**15-30 seconds:**
1. Customer Introduction (3-5s) - Who they are
2. Their Problem (5-8s) - What they struggled with
3. How Product Helped (10-12s) - Transformation
4. CTA (3-5s) - Try it yourself

**30-60 seconds:**
1. Customer Introduction (5-8s) - Who they are, credibility
2. Their Problem (10-15s) - What they struggled with
3. How Product Helped (15-20s) - Detailed transformation
4. Results (8-12s) - Specific outcomes
5. CTA (5s) - Try it yourself

**60-90 seconds:**
1. Customer Introduction (8-12s) - Who they are, background
2. Their Problem (15-20s) - Detailed pain points
3. Discovery (8-12s) - How they found the product
4. How Product Helped (20-25s) - Detailed transformation
5. Results (12-15s) - Specific measurable outcomes
6. CTA (5-8s) - Try it yourself
